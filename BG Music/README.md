# Background music

Drop audio files here (`.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.wav`, `.ogg`, `.flac`, `.opus`) and
they show up in the Shorts Studio music picker automatically — no restart
needed. The file name (without extension) becomes the track name.

Music is mixed onto a rendered video as a post-processing step: the manim
render itself stays silent. Pick a track, choose where in it to start, set the
volume and fades, and it is trimmed to the video's length.

The two `sample_*` files are placeholder tones for testing — delete them once
you have real music in here.
